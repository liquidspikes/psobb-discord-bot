# PSOBB Mag Cells, Evolution Kits & Special Items Guide

## 1. What are Mag Cells & Evolution Kits?
In Phantasy Star Online Blue Burst (PSOBB), **Mag Cells** (often named **"Kit"**, **"Heart"**, **"Parts"**, or **"Cell"**) are special consumable evolutionary items used to transform standard Mags into unique, custom, or Sega-themed Mags (such as **Liberta**, **Chao**, **RoboChao**, **Chu Chu**, **Puyo**, **Opa-Opa**, **Sega Saturn**, etc.).

> **CRITICAL CLARIFICATION FOR HUNTERS:**
> - Mag Cells like the **Liberta Kit** are **NOT monster drops** and do not appear on standard enemy drop charts (they will not show up in `/y/data/rare-table` or monster drop searches).
> - They are **NOT website/client mod kits** or texture downloads.
> - They are genuine in-game item consumables designed to evolve a Mag.

---

## 2. How to Acquire Mag Cells & Evolution Kits on PSOBB.IO

On PSOBB.IO, Mag Cells, Evolution Kits, and endgame utility items are distributed through specific server systems:

### A. Hunter's Guild Bounty Board (https://psobb.io/missions)
- High-tier personal and team bounties (particularly Episode 1 & 2 boss carries, diverse party boss defeats, and hardcore directives) award rare item packages that frequently include Mag Cells, high-grade units, and Photon Spheres.
- Example: Carrying lower-level players through Vol Opt, Dark Falz, or Olga Flow on Ultimate can roll **Liberta Kit**, **Heaven Striker Coat**, or **Tablet** as direct bounty completion rewards.

### B. Level Milestone Claims (https://psobb.io/)
- When characters reach major level milestones, players can claim reward packages on the website dashboard.
- If the player selects the **"Random"** or special reward category:
  - **Level 120+ (Tiers 9 & 10):** Rolls high-tier prizes including **Liberta Kit**, **Tablet**, **Heaven Striker Coat**, **Photon Spheres**, **Photon Crystals**, **Red Ring**, and top-tier units (V101, V502, Heavenly/Battle, Heavenly/Power, Adept).
  - **Level 90–120 (Tiers 7 & 8):** Rolls mid-to-high tier cells including **Pioneer Parts**, **D-Photon Core**, **Yahoo!s Engine**, and materials.
  - **Level 60–90 (Tiers 5 & 6):** Rolls entry cells including **Amities Memo** (Chu Chu) and **Heart of Morolian**.

### C. Server Events & Holiday Quests
- Seasonal events (Halloween, Christmas, Anniversary) and special event shops hosted in Pioneer 2 lobbies often allow trading Photon Drops (PDs) or event badges for Mag Cells.

## 4. How to Use a Mag Cell / Evolution Kit
1. Ensure your Mag meets the level requirement (usually Level 50+ or Level 100+).
2. Ensure your character has the cell item in their inventory.
3. Open the **Item Pack**, select the Mag Cell / Kit item, and choose **Use**.
4. Target the eligible Mag. The Mag will instantly evolve into its new rare form!
5. **Stats & Photon Blasts:** The newly evolved Cell Mag retains its current DEF, POW, DEX, and MIND stats, as well as any previously learned Photon Blasts (like Mylla & Youlla).
